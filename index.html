Xu Meng 许萌

I am currently a Lecturer and a Master's Supervisor at Beijing University of Technology (BJUT), China. My research focuses on brain-computer interfaces, video/image-based target recognition, embodied intelligence, and trustworthy and secure large language models.

Research Interests

My research interests include brain-computer interfaces, video/image-based target recognition, embodied intelligence, and trustworthy and secure large language models.

I have participated in national and Beijing municipal research projects. Over the past five years, I have published more than 30 SCI/EI papers, obtained four national invention patents granted or under examination and one software copyright. I received second place nationally in the Youth Paper Competition of the BCI Brain-Controlled Robot Contest at the World Robot Competition.
